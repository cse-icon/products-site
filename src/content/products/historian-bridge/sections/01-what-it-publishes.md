  - title: Tag metadata
    description: A daily snapshot of every tag and its properties, with the ones you pick promoted to columns.
  - title: Events
    description: Canary Calcs & Events occurrences, with open events kept current until they close.
  - title: Quality preserved
    description: Bad and uncertain readings keep their quality codes, so your analysts decide what to filter.
  - title: Configuration, not code
    description: Adding a source takes a Canary view, a scoped token, and one config entry.

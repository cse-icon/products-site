  - title: Private by Default
    description: The most common layout listens only on your private network, and each user connects through a small local proxy.
  - title: Reach From Anywhere
    description: For claude.ai and ChatGPT, publish over HTTPS on one port, or through a Cloudflare Tunnel with no inbound port at all.
  - title: Local or Central
    description: Run one shared server, or one per analyst laptop for maximum isolation.
  - title: Monitoring Built In
    description: Health, statistics, and metrics endpoints, plus OpenTelemetry, for your existing monitoring stack.
  - title: Offline Licensing
    description: One licence per server, checked on the machine itself. Nothing calls home.
